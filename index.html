01. About

I'm a junior IT developer who likes working close to the data layer — building backend logic in PHP, keeping legacy databases healthy, and making sure systems that other people depend on don't quietly break in production. I'm drawn to problems with real constraints: thin documentation, live systems you can't just take down, and data that has to come out the other side correct, not just fast. Most of my hands-on experience so far has come from maintaining and extending internal backend systems and helping migrate a production database from Oracle to MariaDB — the kind of work that rewards patience and double-checking over cleverness.
